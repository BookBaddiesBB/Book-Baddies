📦 Book Baddies – Returns & Refunds

Last updated: September 2026

Please note: This policy does not remove or limit any consumer rights that apply to you under South African law.

1. Our Approach

We want you to be happy with your Book Baddies order. If there is a problem with your order, please contact us as soon as possible so we can investigate and assist you.

2. Damaged Products

If your order arrives damaged, please contact Book Baddies as soon as reasonably possible after receiving the parcel.

Where possible, please provide:

  • Your name and order details
  • Photographs of the damaged item
  • Photographs of the packaging
  • A description of what happened

We will assess the issue and determine the appropriate remedy in accordance with applicable consumer law.

3. Defective or Faulty Products

If a product is defective or does not meet the applicable legal requirements, please contact us so that we can assess the issue.

Where statutory consumer protections apply, Book Baddies will provide the remedy required by applicable law.

Depending on the circumstances and applicable legal requirements, this may include repair, replacement or refund.

4. Incorrect Item

If you receive an item that is different from the product you ordered, please contact us as soon as possible.

We will investigate the order and, where Book Baddies supplied the incorrect item, work with you to arrange an appropriate solution.

5. Missing Items

If something appears to be missing from your order, please contact us with your order information.

We will check the order and packing information and assist with resolving the issue.

6. Lost or Delayed Parcels

If your parcel is delayed or appears to have been lost, please contact Book Baddies.

We will provide available tracking information and, where necessary, assist with a courier investigation.

Any refund, replacement or other remedy will be handled in accordance with the applicable consumer-protection laws and the circumstances of the order.

7. Change-of-Mind Returns

Where a customer simply changes their mind, a return or refund is not automatically guaranteed unless a statutory right or a Book Baddies offer applies.

If you wish to request a change-of-mind return, please contact us before sending anything back. We will advise whether the particular product and transaction qualify for a return and what process applies.

8. Books and Special Editions

Books, collector's editions and other special products may have particular return conditions depending on the product, its condition and applicable law.

Normal characteristics of a product, such as minor manufacturing variations or differences between photographs and the physical item, do not necessarily mean that a product is defective.

9. Returns Process

Please do not send an item back without first contacting Book Baddies.

We will provide the relevant return instructions where a return is appropriate.

10. Refunds

Where a refund is due, the refund method and timing will depend on the circumstances of the transaction, the payment method and applicable law.

We will communicate the relevant refund arrangements with the customer directly.

11. Consumer Rights

Nothing in this Returns & Refunds Policy is intended to exclude, restrict or waive any right or remedy that a consumer has under applicable South African law.
