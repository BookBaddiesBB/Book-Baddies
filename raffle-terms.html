🎟️ Book Baddies – Raffle Terms & Conditions

Last updated: September 2026

Important: Each individual raffle may have additional rules displayed on its promotional post or raffle graphic. Those rules should be read together with these terms.

1. How Book Baddies Raffles Work

Book Baddies may occasionally run paid-entry raffles where participants purchase numbered entries for the opportunity to win the advertised prize.

The number of available entries, entry price, prize and opening date will be communicated for each individual raffle.

2. Entry Price

Unless otherwise stated on the specific raffle advertisement, the entry price is:

R10 per ticket.

Participants may purchase multiple tickets where tickets are available.

3. Payment

Participants must make payment using the payment instructions provided by Book Baddies.

Proof of payment must be sent according to the instructions provided for that raffle.

Proof of payment should clearly show the participant's name or identifying information and the number of tickets purchased where requested.

4. Ticket Confirmation

A ticket is only regarded as confirmed once Book Baddies has received and verified the relevant payment.

Participants should not assume that a ticket is reserved until confirmation has been provided.

5. Selling Out the Raffle

The raffle draw will take place once the required number of tickets has been sold, unless the specific raffle advertisement states otherwise.

6. The Draw

Book Baddies intends to conduct the draw fairly and transparently.

Where a spinning-wheel or similar random-selection method is used, the draw process may be recorded or shown to the community.

The winning ticket will be selected from the valid paid entries for that raffle.

7. Winner

The winner will be contacted using the contact information supplied when entering the raffle.

The winner may be asked to provide the information reasonably required to arrange delivery of the prize.

8. Prize Delivery

Where the prize is a physical item, Book Baddies will arrange delivery using the delivery method communicated for that raffle.

The winner must provide accurate delivery information.

9. Prize Substitution

Book Baddies will make reasonable efforts to provide the advertised prize.

If circumstances outside Book Baddies' reasonable control make the advertised prize unavailable, Book Baddies will communicate with affected participants and provide an appropriate solution subject to applicable law.

10. Free Tickets and Promotions

From time to time, Book Baddies may offer promotions such as:

  • Buy 5 tickets and receive 1 free ticket;
  • Bonus entries;
  • Special promotional offers; or
  • Other entry incentives.

Any promotional offer will be subject to the specific conditions communicated when the promotion is announced.

11. Invalid or Unverified Entries

Book Baddies may exclude an entry where payment cannot be verified or where the entry does not comply with the applicable raffle rules, subject always to applicable law.

12. Refunds and Cancelled Raffles

If a raffle needs to be cancelled, materially changed or cannot proceed, Book Baddies will communicate with participants regarding the available remedy in accordance with applicable law.

13. Age and Eligibility

Eligibility requirements may differ between raffles and will be stated in the relevant raffle announcement where necessary.

14. Community Rules

Participants are expected to communicate respectfully within the Book Baddies community.

Raffle participation does not give anyone the right to harass, threaten or abuse Book Baddies, other participants or community members.

15. No Guarantee of Winning

Purchasing additional tickets increases the number of entries held by a participant but does not guarantee that participant will win.

16. Changes to Raffle Rules

Any material changes to an individual raffle will be communicated to participants as soon as reasonably possible and handled in accordance with applicable law.

17. South African Law

These raffle terms are intended to operate under the laws of the Republic of South Africa.

Nothing in these terms is intended to exclude or limit any mandatory legal right.
