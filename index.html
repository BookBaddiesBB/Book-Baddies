The person behind Book Baddies

Hi! I'm Mic, the owner and creator behind Book Baddies.

I created Book Baddies because I wanted to create something that could connect the book people of South Africa.

Whether you're obsessed with fantasy worlds, completely addicted to romance, love a good mystery, collect beautiful editions or simply enjoy chatting about books, there is a place here for you.

Book Baddies is about bringing readers together, discovering new stories, sharing our favourite books, having fun with raffles and creating a little bookish community that feels like home.

So whether you're here to buy a ticket, order a book box, recommend a book or simply join the chatter... you are welcome here. ♡
