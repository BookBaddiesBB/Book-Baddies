📚 Book Baddies – Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Your Privacy

Book Baddies respects your privacy and aims to handle your personal information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you contact Book Baddies, place an order or interact with our website.

2. Information We May Collect

When you place an order, we may need information such as:

  • Your name
  • Your WhatsApp number
  • Your delivery information
  • Your selected PUDO locker
  • Your order details
  • Information you voluntarily provide in your order notes

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information may be used to:

  • Process and manage your order
  • Communicate with you about your order
  • Arrange delivery
  • Deal with customer-service queries
  • Manage refunds, returns or product issues
  • Communicate with raffle winners where applicable

4. WhatsApp

When you choose to place an order through the website, the information entered into the order form is used to create a WhatsApp message to Book Baddies.

WhatsApp is an external service and its own privacy terms and policies may apply to information processed through the platform.

5. Sharing Information

We will only share customer information where reasonably necessary for purposes such as fulfilling an order, arranging delivery, dealing with a payment or addressing a legal requirement.

6. Payment Information

Customers should not enter passwords, banking PINs, card PINs or other highly confidential financial information into the website order form or WhatsApp order message.

7. Keeping Information Safe

Book Baddies takes reasonable steps to protect personal information from unauthorised access, loss or misuse.

8. Your Rights

You may contact Book Baddies if you have questions about personal information that we hold about you or if you believe your information has been handled incorrectly.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary to reflect changes to the Book Baddies website, services or applicable legal requirements.
